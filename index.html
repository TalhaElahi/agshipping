ABOUT US

Your Trusted Global
Logistics Partner

AG Shipping, established in 2004, has grown as a leading international Freight Forwarder in Pakistan serving the industry over a decade in Pakistan, UAE & UK, USA & Canada market for entire range of Logistics & Freight services including Air Freight, Ocean Freight, SCM & Distribution, Customs Clearance, Courier, Moving & Packing services all over Pakistan and worldwide.

Our presence spans across key markets in Asia, Middle East, Europe, and North America, with dedicated offices in Dubai, London, and strategic locations in Pakistan, enabling us to serve our global clientele with seamless logistics solutions.

Over the past two decades, our expert team has successfully handled diverse shipping projects across continents, specializing in both standard freight services and complex logistics challenges. We take pride in our extensive network of trusted partners worldwide.
